## Verified information
Flyerz offers back-window advertising printed on perforated Contravision, with Applied Contravision and DIY Contravision options. Quantities offered: 1 and 2.

The shop lists the Vehicle Branding category at R450–R1,499 excluding VAT. This range also includes car magnets (a set of two, each 500 × 300 mm). Do not treat the range endpoints as verified Contravision variant prices or as per-square-metre rates.

Exact prices for quantity 1/2, DIY/fitted, window size limits, installation arrangements, delivery, and design charges remain unverified. Confirm these before creating a supplier price rule. The product page says supplier-provided artwork design adds two business days.
